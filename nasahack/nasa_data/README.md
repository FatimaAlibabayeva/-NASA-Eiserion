# Real NASA study data used by Eiserion

`lettuce_elements.csv` transcribes the Ca, Mg and K rows from Table 7 of the peer-reviewed article linked below. The paper reports measurements from the [NASA OSDR lettuce study](https://osdr.nasa.gov/bio/repo/data/studies/OSD-267), one of the experiments underlying the [Data.gov dataset supplied for this project](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/microbiological-and-nutritional-analysis-of-lettuce-crops-grown-on-the-international-space). The paper's data availability statement identifies VEG-01A as GLDS-267, VEG-01B as GLDS-268, and VEG-03A as GLDS-269. These are **published study means**, transcribed into a small local CSV, not individual raw sample records.

Primary table: [Khodadad et al., *Frontiers in Plant Science* (2020), Table 7](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/plant-science/articles/10.3389/fpls.2020.00199/pdf). Values are mg per gram of **dry lettuce leaf tissue**, mean ± standard error, n=3. `Flight` means ISS-grown lettuce; `Ground` means matching ground controls. The original table reports that, within each experiment, flight and ground elemental values were not significantly different. Do not read the displayed differences as a spaceflight effect.

No dry-to-fresh conversion, consumed serving mass, or astronaut intake is available in this table. Eiserion therefore displays these observations as a separate source-backed comparison and **does not** inject them into the mock daily intake or adaptive planning equations. The user-entered profile, nutrition, exercise and QUS remain simulated.

NASA's [Food and Nutrition technical brief](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/ochmo-tb-013-food-and-nutrition.pdf) is a reference document, not a measurement dataset. It emphasizes age- and sex-matched dietary reference intakes and individualized crew needs. The two other linked NLSP catalog pages were not accessible with verifiable numerical records, so their figures are not represented here.
